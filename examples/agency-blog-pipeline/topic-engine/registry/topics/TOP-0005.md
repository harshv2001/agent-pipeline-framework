## Why this is a gap

The site repeatedly promises a P&L and balance sheet "every month" but
never explains, for a reader without a finance background, what those
two reports actually show or why reviewing them monthly matters — a
natural companion piece once a prospective client already understands
the reconciliation cadence (existing post) and before they'd need the
Fractional CFO-level advisory content. Lower priority than TOP-0001/0002
since it supports rather than fills a named-service gap.
