## Post body

<p>Building a 13-week cash flow forecast takes four steps: list your
expected cash in, list your expected cash out, roll it forward week by
week, and update it as real numbers come in.</p>

<h2>Step 1: List what's coming in</h2>
<p>Start with every source of cash you expect over the next 13 weeks —
invoices due, recurring payments, anything else.</p>

<h2>Step 2: List what's going out</h2>
<p>Do the same for cash leaving — payroll, rent, suppliers, loan
payments, taxes.</p>

<h2>Step 3: Roll it forward week by week</h2>
<p>Put both lists into a simple weekly grid so you can see the running
balance change over time.</p>

<h2>Step 4: Update it as reality happens</h2>
<p>Swap in actual numbers as the weeks pass so the forecast stays
accurate.</p>
