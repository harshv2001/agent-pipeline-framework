How Often Should a Small Business Reconcile Its Books?

Monthly, at minimum — and here's why waiting until tax season is the single most expensive habit a small business can have.

What "reconciling" actually means

Matching every transaction in your bank and card statements against what's recorded in your books, every month, so small errors get caught while they're still small.

The cost of waiting

A business that reconciles once a year doesn't just do twelve months of work at once — they do it blind, months after the context that would explain a weird charge is gone.

What good monthly reconciliation looks like

A closed P&L and balance sheet within the first two weeks of the following month, every month, with nothing left "to figure out later."
