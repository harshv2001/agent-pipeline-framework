5 Signs You Need a Fractional CFO (Not a Full-Time One)

A full-time CFO costs more than most 15-person companies spend on their whole finance function. Here's how to tell you've outgrown a bookkeeper without needing to hire one.

  1. You're guessing, not forecasting, when someone asks "can we afford this hire?"
  2. Board or investor updates take a scramble, not a standing deck.
  3. Cash is fine on average but tight in specific months, and you don't know which ones are coming.
  4. You've made a pricing or hiring decision you later wished you'd run the numbers on first.
  5. Your bookkeeper is excellent at the books and has no opinion on strategy — which is exactly their job, and exactly the gap.
