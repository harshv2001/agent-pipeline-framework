About ClientA

ClientA started because our founder kept hearing the same sentence from small-business owners: "I know my books are a mess, I just don't have time to fix them." So we built the thing we wished existed — bookkeeping and finance help that's sized for a five-to-thirty-person company, not a Fortune 500.

How we talk

Direct, warm, a little informal. We'd rather say "you're spending more than you're bringing in" than "there is a negative variance in operating cash flow." Numbers are stressful enough without finance-speak on top.

Our promise

No surprises at tax time, no mystery line items, and a monthly call where you leave understanding your own business better than when you called in.
